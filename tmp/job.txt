1. Reconstruct the task metadata using the LLM. This can run pre-experiment or serve as a set-up phase before task execution and diffusion. In this case we do not solely rely on a weak signal of difficulty.
2. Compact the artifact store similarly before skill update reflection. This, along with other task metadata, can be consumed by the agent to determine the diffusion direction.
3. At the diffusion checkpoint, the agent can choose up to k (<=3) tasks to diffuse, each with a similarity index decided by the agent. Since past artifacts are also included, this similarity will depend not only on the task's nature but also on their past run results, such as failure reasons. At different iterations, a task will also have a chance to shuffle the diffusion directions. Once the diffusion operation is complete, the consumed artifacts are dropped and will no longer be used, but they will be kept for audit.
4. The similarity index of the k tasks passes through a softmax gate, which generates a probability distribution used to determine the single target task activated by this source task.
5. Source tasks will not run in the iteration unless another source task activates them for the next round.
