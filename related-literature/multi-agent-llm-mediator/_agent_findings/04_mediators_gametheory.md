# 调研报告: Mediators & Mediated Communication (game-theoretic / MARL / LLM)

This slice traces the full lineage of "a third party that routes/filters/recommends information to coordinate agents" — from Aumann's correlated equilibrium through learned MARL mediators to LLM-era AI mediators. All items verified via search.

## 补充Items

### Foundational theory (game-theoretic-mediator / mediated-equilibrium)
- **Subjectivity and Correlation in Randomized Strategies** (1974, R. Aumann): origin of **correlated equilibrium** — a correlation device/mediator sends private signals so the obedient strategy profile is a Nash equilibrium of the augmented game; achieves payoffs outside the Nash convex hull. https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-4068(74)90037-8
- **Correlated Equilibrium as an Expression of Bayesian Rationality** (1987, R. Aumann): re-grounds CE as Bayesian rationality with a common prior; the mediator is an information partition, no explicit randomization needed. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1911154
- **An Approach to Communication Equilibria** (1986, F. Forges, Econometrica 54(6):1375–1385): defines **communication equilibrium** — a mediator that both *receives* messages (inputs) from players and *sends* recommendations; the canonical "two-way" mediator for incomplete-information/multistage games. Also introduces autonomous correlated equilibrium. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1914304
- **Multistage Games with Communication** (1986, R. Myerson, Econometrica): parallel formalization of mediated/communication equilibria + revelation principle for dynamic games; the mediator as a trusted recommender with memory.
- **Strategic Information Transmission** (1982, V. Crawford & J. Sobel, Econometrica 50(6):1431–1451): **cheap talk** — un-mediated, costless, non-binding signaling; partition (interval) equilibria; informativeness decreasing in preference misalignment. The "no-mediator / no-commitment" baseline against which mediation is contrasted. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1913390
- **Bayesian Persuasion** (2011, E. Kamenica & M. Gentzkow, AER): **information design** — a sender commits to a signaling scheme to optimally shape a receiver's beliefs (concavification). The KEY mediation-and-information-filtering primitive: commitment + obedience constraints. (See also Bergemann & Morris, "Information Design: A Unified Perspective," 2019, JEL — generalizes to many receivers / Bayes correlated equilibrium.)
- **Program Equilibrium** (2004, M. Tennenholtz, GEB 49:363–373): players submit programs that read each other's source; achieves cooperation in one-shot PD. Mediator-free conditional commitment; program-eq payoffs = individually-rational feasible set. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geb.2004.02.002
- **K-Implementation** (2004, D. Monderer & M. Tennenholtz, JAIR 21:37–62): an "interested party" implements outcomes via committed *non-negative* transfers that need not materialize — a payment-based mediator/incentive designer. https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.0022
- **Strong Mediated Equilibrium** (2009, D. Monderer & M. Tennenholtz, Artificial Intelligence): the strongest action-mediator notion — a reliable party that can **act on behalf of** consenting agents (not just recommend), giving stability against coalition deviations; also "routing mediators" (Ashlagi-Monderer-Rozenfeld). This is the direct theoretical ancestor of MARL mediators. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2008.10.005
- **A Commitment Folk Theorem** (Kalai, Kalai, Lehrer & Samet, GEB 2010): commitment devices generalize program equilibrium; characterizes attainable payoffs. http://www.math.tau.ac.il/~lehrer/Papers/commitment%20folk%20theorem.pdf

### Computational / equilibrium-computation mediators
- **Polynomial-Time Optimal Equilibria with a Mediator in Extensive-Form Games** (2022, B.H. Zhang & T. Sandholm): **mediator-augmented games** — a single construction where varying the mediator's information partition / players' ability to lie/deviate recovers correlated, communication (Forges), and certification (Forges-Koessler) equilibria; EFCE = mediator with imperfect recall. The unifying computational backbone for learned mediators. https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.15395
- **Computing Optimal Equilibria and Mechanisms via Learning in Zero-Sum Extensive-Form Games** (2023, B.H. Zhang, Farina, Anagnostides, … Conitzer, Sandholm, NeurIPS): casts optimal-mediator/mechanism design as a zero-sum game (mediator vs. deviation-finder), solvable with deep-RL game solvers (PSRO); covers correlated/communication/certification equilibria + mechanism & information design. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05216

### MARL mediators (MARL-mediator / learned)
- **Learning to Incentivize Other Learning Agents (LIO)** (2020, Yang, Li, Farajtabar, Sunehag, Hughes, Zha, NeurIPS): an agent learns an *incentive (reward-gifting) function* over others' actions via online cross-validation through their learning steps — a learned payment-mediator (k-implementation in RL). https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/ad7ed5d47b9baceb12045a929e7e2f66-Paper.pdf
- **Improving Social Welfare While Preserving Autonomy via a Pareto Mediator** (2021, McAleer, Lanier, Dennis, Baldi, Fox): a *voluntary* mediator — agents submit an action + a delegate-bit; the mediator reassigns delegating agents' actions for a Pareto improvement; degrades gracefully under model misspecification. Explicitly contrasts CE-recommenders vs. acting-mediators. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03927
- **Mediated Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning** (2023, D. Ivanov, I. Zisman, K. Chernyshev, AAMAS): trains a **mediator jointly with agents via policy gradient** to maximize social welfare subject to incentive constraints (Lagrangian / dual gradient descent) so committing-through-the-mediator is an equilibrium; introduces "Markov mediators." The central MARL-mediator paper for this project. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08419
- **Coordinating Policies via an Intelligent Communication Channel / Stateful Active Facilitator (SAF)** (2022, D. Liu, V. Shah, Boussif, Meo, A. Goyal, Shu, Mozer, Heess, Bengio): **communication-as-mediation** — agents don't talk directly; a learned stateful facilitator (memory slots + key-value attention) filters/interprets all signals and broadcasts; agents are incentivized to *reduce* dependence so it doesn't become a central controller; messages select among policies, not actions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10607 (and HECOGrid: 2210.03022)
- **Information Design in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning** (2023, Y. Lin, W. Li, H. Zha, B. Wang, NeurIPS): formalizes the **Markov Signaling Game** with a learned **signaling gradient** + extended obedience constraints — Bayesian-persuasion mediator made learnable in sequential mixed-motive RL. Code: https://github.com/YueLin301/InformationDesignMARL · https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06807
- **Markov Persuasion Process / OP4** (Wu, Zhang, Feng, Wang et al., 2022, ICML): provably-efficient no-regret RL for a sender's *signaling policy* (learning to persuade in a sequential environment). https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.10678 ; **Sequential Information Design: Learning to Persuade in the Dark** (Bernasconi, Castiglioni, Celli, Marchesi, Trovò, Gatti, NeurIPS 2022) — online persuasion with bandit feedback; **Online Bayesian Persuasion Without a Clue** (NeurIPS 2024).
- **Principal-Agent RL: Orchestrating AI Agents with Contracts** (2024, Ivanov, Gerstgrasser, et al.): deep-RL principal designs *contracts* (outcome-based payments) guiding agent policies; converges to subgame-perfect equilibrium; scales to sequential social dilemmas (Coin Game). Mediator-as-contract-designer. https://arxiv.org/html/2407.18074 ; related **Formal Contracts Mitigate Social Dilemmas in MARL / MOCA** (Christoffersen et al., AAMAS/JAAMAS 2024) https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10458-024-09682-5
- **Learning Recommender Mechanisms for Bayesian Stochastic Games** (2025): bi-level RL learns a *recommender mechanism* (meta-policy over type profiles) with differentiable IC/IR losses (straight-through Gumbel-softmax); bridges one-shot mediators (Myerson, Monderer-Tennenholtz) and EFG correlation (Zhang-Sandholm) to Bayesian stochastic games. https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22979
- **AgentMixer: Correlated Policy Factorization** (2025, AAAI): a Policy Modifier acts as a mediator/observer that turns decentralized partially-observable policies into a correlated joint policy, provably converging to ε-approximate **correlated equilibrium** with decentralized execution. https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/34048/36203
- **Emergence of Fair Leaders via Mediators in MARL (JAM-QL)** (2025): integrates Markov mediators (per Ivanov) into Stackelberg games with dynamic leaders; mediator constrains leader selection to maximize fairness. Useful recent confirmation/extension of the Markov-mediator line. https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02421
- **MEDIATE** (Mutually Endorsed Distributed Incentive Acknowledgment Token Exchange) — peer-incentive/token-exchange communication protocol for emergent cooperation in sequential social dilemmas (Phan et al.; recently ported to quantum MARL, 2026). A decentralized "mediation via mutual acknowledgment" mechanism. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18419

### LLM-era mediators (LLM-mediator, 2023–2026)
- **Habermas Machine** — "AI can help humans find common ground in democratic deliberation" (2024, Tessler, Bakker, Jarrett, Sheahan, … Parkes, Botvinick, Summerfield, *Science* 386:eadq2852): LLM trained (SFT + reward model over group-approval) to iteratively generate "group statements" capturing common ground across human discussants; preferred over human mediators 56% vs 44%; incorporates dissent while respecting majority. The flagship AI-mediator-of-humans. Code/dataset: https://github.com/google-deepmind/habermas_machine · https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adq2852
- **ProMediate** (2025): first eval framework for *proactive* AI mediators in multi-party negotiation; plug-and-play mediator decides *when* and *how* to intervene; socio-cognitive metrics (consensus/efficiency, intervention tempo). https://arxiv.org/html/2510.25224
- **LLM-Deliberation / scorable negotiation games** (Abdelnabi, Gomaa, Sivaprasad, Schönherr, Fritz, ICLR-W 2024 / NeurIPS D&B 2024): multi-agent, multi-issue negotiation benchmark with cooperation/competition/maliciousness; relevant for LLM agents negotiating with/without a mediator. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/984dd3db213db2d1454a163b65b84d08-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.pdf
- **The Concordia Contest** (Smith, Trivedi, Clifton, Hammond, … Jaques, Baarslag, Leibo et al., NeurIPS 2024 competition): advancing cooperative intelligence of LM agents in mixed-motive settings — directly relevant to coevolving cooperative/mediating LLM agents. https://openreview.net/pdf?id=dfeFy1PSSw
- **Multi-Agent-Debate moderators** (LLM-orchestrator-as-mediator): Du et al. "Improving Factuality and Reasoning through Multiagent Debate" (ICML 2024); **MACI** (2025, arXiv:2510.04488) — an active *moderator* with dual dials (information-quality gate + contentiousness) tracking disagreement/overlap/evidence and provably terminating; **CortexDebate** (ACL Findings 2025) — moderator optimizes a sparse dynamic debate graph (mediator that *routes* who-talks-to-whom). These are LLM mediators coordinating *other LLM agents*. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.04488
- **Agent Q-Mix** (2026, arXiv:2604.00344): casts LLM-multi-agent *communication-topology* learning as cooperative MARL (QMIX) — each agent picks a comm action (broadcast/query/debate/verify), inducing the routing graph. Direct bridge between learned-comm-routing mediation and LLM agents. https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2604.00344
- **LLM-mediated negotiation in MARL pipelines**: LLM agents as adaptive mediators/negotiators for inter-enterprise coordination over MARL/MAPPO (e.g., cold-chain logistics, supply-chain consensus-seeking — Jannelli et al. 2025). Shows the LLM-mediator-over-RL-agents pattern in applied multi-agent systems.

## 推荐补充字段
- **incentive_compatibility / obedience**: whether agents are *incentivized to follow* the mediator's recommendation (CE obedience constraints, extended obedience in MSG, IC losses). The defining constraint separating a true mediator from a central controller. Values: none / ex-post-IC / approximate / Lagrangian-soft.
- **commitment_assumption**: does the mediator commit to its scheme/policy ex ante (Bayesian persuasion, information design) vs. cheap-talk (no commitment) vs. emergent-via-reputation (RL). Critical axis — distinguishes persuasion from cheap talk.
- **mediator_power**: recommend-only (correlated/communication eq, recommender mechanisms) vs. act-on-behalf-of (strong mediated eq, Pareto Mediator) vs. pay/contract (k-implementation, LIO, principal-agent contracts) vs. inform-only (Bayesian persuasion / signaling). Captures the strength hierarchy.
- **information_asymmetry / what_the_mediator_knows**: omniscient (Bayes correlated eq) vs. report-dependent (communication eq, Forges-Myerson) vs. observation-only. Determines the equilibrium class achievable.
- **voluntariness / participation**: mandatory routing vs. voluntary delegation (agents choose whether to use the mediator) — key in Pareto Mediator, Mediated MARL, SAF.
- **truthfulness / cheap-talk-vs-binding**: are messages binding/verifiable (certification eq), costless-and-non-binding (cheap talk), or payment-backed (k-implementation)? Sub-field of mediation mechanism.
- **welfare_objective**: social-welfare-max vs. fairness (JAM-QL) vs. sender-optimal (persuasion) vs. Pareto-improvement-constrained — what the mediator optimizes.
- **learnability_of_mediator**: fixed/hand-designed vs. learned-jointly-with-agents vs. learned-against-best-responder (zero-sum reformulation) vs. co-evolved. Maps directly onto the existing `adaptivity` field but specialized to the mediator role — and is the crux of "mediator-coevo."
- **non_stationarity_handling**: how the framework copes with agents adapting to the mediator (signaling gradient through receiver learning, online cross-validation in LIO, bi-level RL) — a defining technical challenge for *learned* mediators.

**Key synthesis for mediator-coevo**: There is a clean conceptual ladder — *correlation device* (Aumann) → *communication mediator* (Forges/Myerson) → *information designer* (Kamenica-Gentzkow) → *acting/payment mediator* (Monderer-Tennenholtz) → *computational mediator-augmented games* (Zhang-Sandholm) → *learned MARL mediators* (Ivanov, LIO, SAF, signaling-gradient, principal-agent-RL) → *LLM mediators* (Habermas Machine, ProMediate, debate moderators). The most direct precedents for **co-evolving** a mediator with agents are Ivanov's Mediated MARL (joint policy-gradient training with incentive constraints) and the zero-sum "mediator vs. deviation-finder" reformulation (Zhang-Farina 2023) — both explicitly train the mediator *against* adaptive agents, which is the coevolutionary core of this project.
